# Answer-OPD pilot data audit

## Frozen first-stage pool

The current first stage is deliberately an **8-configuration, 32-condition** diagnostic. It uses `H01`, `H04`, `H07`, `H13`, `H14`, `H15`, `DA01`, and `DA05`, with four parameter variants (`v0`–`v3`) per configuration. The 32 rows contain 8 originals, 18 `shape` variants, and 6 `similarity` variants. The student sees the English numerical geometry problem and is asked to derive and justify its exact value. The teacher sees the identical prompt with one additional numeric-answer hint. The only training reference is the exact scalar; neither a general relation nor a worked solution enters the pool.

The frozen files are [train-pool.json](train-pool.json), [eval-jobs.json](eval-jobs.json), and [eval-answer-key.json](eval-answer-key.json). [prepare_data.py](prepare_data.py) reconstructs them from the canonical JSONL and English template sources; [data-freeze.json](data-freeze.json) records input and output hashes. Each pool row has `id`, `source_id`, `variant`, `reference`, `student_messages`, and `teacher_messages`; augmentation, givens, and source hashes are audit metadata. For example, the H01 v0 row has reference `32/5`, givens `BC=12, AM=10`, and the two user-message contents differ only by `Verified exact numeric-answer hint: 32/5`.

The source labels are `answer_exact` in [training_variants.jsonl](../../../benchmarks/humpty_configurations_v2/training_variants.jsonl). English problem text is rendered from `NUMERIC_EN` in [english_templates.py](../qwen35-diverse-sft-2026-09-24/english_templates.py), using the same row's `givens`. The existing [prepare_english.py](../qwen35-diverse-sft-2026-09-24/prepare_english.py) checks placeholder agreement and renders English jobs; it uses a proof-and-relation request for its previous study, so the new pilot builder supplies its own reasoning prompt while retaining the checked numerical templates. The pool does not read `answer` from the older full-solution training files.

## What the existing corpus supports

[training_variants.jsonl](../../../benchmarks/humpty_configurations_v2/training_variants.jsonl) contains 148 rows across 37 configurations: 37 originals, 27 `shape`, and 84 `similarity`. Therefore, the full file has 64 original-or-shape rows, not 128 distinct nonsimilar examples. Only nine configurations have shape variants (`H01`, `H02`, `H03`, `H04`, `H07`, `H13`, `H14`, `H15`, `HX01`). For a 12–16 configuration subset, the maximum available original-plus-shape count is 39–43; it cannot supply 128 nonsimilar instances without generating more numeric instances or admitting similarity variants. The current 32-row pool has 26 original-plus-shape rows and six similarity rows. A later 512-rollout schedule over these 32 rows represents repeated conditions, not 512 unique problems.

[variant_validation.json](../../../benchmarks/humpty_configurations_v2/variant_validation.json) records zero numerical duplicates and those augmentation counts; it also cautions that `shape` does not certify pairwise non-similarity across every example. [verification_report.json](../../../benchmarks/humpty_configurations_v2/verification_report.json) reports 40 accepted numerical construction examples and explicitly limits its scope to original constructions, supplied givens, and target values. It states that this was not a repository-solver run. The accepted labels are suitable for this learning-method diagnostic, but they must not be described as solver-generated labels. The [research correction](../intent-realignment-2026-09-26/CORRECTION.md) separately records direct `geosolver.solve` checks for three original examples; it does not establish a 148-row solver pipeline.

## Proof and numeric evaluation

The 24 frozen evaluation jobs comprise eight literal same-configuration general claims, each run at two fixed seeds; two secondary cross-configuration proof prompts (`EV02`, `OB01`), also at two seeds; and four elementary exact-value sentinels, each at one seed. Expected claims and numeric values are isolated in `eval-answer-key.json`, not in model messages. The elementary prompts ask for a short justification and have exact values `2√7`, `15/2`, `5`, and `6`.

The eight primary proof claims are one per trained configuration: H01 `AM·MP=MB²`; H04 `AT²=TB·TC`; H07 `[EPQ]=AB·MF·(1−AB²/(4·ON²))`; H13 `Z∈AC`; H14 `AR⊥HR`; H15 `K` is the midpoint of `AD`; DA01 `AT·AM=AB·AC/2`; and DA05 `MD·ME=MB²`. Their assumptions and exact question text are in `eval-jobs.json`. They were checked against `status: accept` records in [audits.json](../qwen35-external-teacher-2026-09-24/audits.json) and the corresponding configuration entries in [catalogue.md](../../../benchmarks/humpty_configurations_v2/catalogue.md). In particular, H07's area identity uses the audited radial-product and reflection relations, with `M` between `O` and `N`; DA01's median identity is equivalent to the audited `AT=bc/√(2b²+2c²−a²)` for a nondegenerate triangle.

Existing English full-proof sources are useful for audit and provenance but are excluded from numeric training: [train.json](../qwen35-external-teacher-2026-09-24/train.json) and [candidates.json](../qwen35-external-teacher-2026-09-24/candidates.json) contain complete teacher solutions. English v1/v2 proof candidates are also present in [h01-v1-v2-candidates.json](../qwen35-external-teacher-2026-09-25/h01-v1-v2-candidates.json), [h07-v1-v2-candidates.json](../qwen35-external-teacher-2026-09-25/h07-v1-v2-candidates.json), and [h02-h13-v1-v2-candidates.json](../qwen35-external-teacher-2026-09-25/h02-h13-v1-v2-candidates.json). They are not numeric-only teachers. The canonical English numeric translations for each v0–v3 row come from `NUMERIC_EN` plus `givens`, paired directly with current `answer_exact` labels.

The secondary proof prompts are the existing English jobs in [eval-proof-mechanism-jobs.json](../qwen35-external-teacher-2026-09-25/eval-proof-mechanism-jobs.json). Fresh same-configuration proof questions also exist in [target_eval_jobs.json](../qwen35-external-teacher-2026-09-25/heldout-near-transfer/target_eval_jobs.json) and [followup-fresh2/target_eval_jobs.json](../qwen35-external-teacher-2026-09-25/heldout-near-transfer/followup-fresh2/target_eval_jobs.json), but those are prior-study numeric-instance questions for only H03/H04/H08/H14/H15; they were not used for this first-stage theorem-claim evaluation.

## Leakage and scope limits

The primary proof claims are intentionally same-configuration targets, while the numeric training pool repeats those configurations with different givens. This measures within-configuration generalization and proof transfer, not independent-configuration discovery. H01 and H04 share the orthocenter-projection machinery; report them as a related pair. The claims were selected using existing audited relation metadata, and EV02/OB01 are reused development prompts, so the result is exploratory rather than an untouched confirmatory evaluation.

The English templates were manually authored translations. Placeholder agreement and absence of Japanese script do not prove that every geometric nuance or branch condition survived translation; any causal claim beyond this pilot needs bilingual semantic review. The full-solution candidate files remain read-only audit sources and must not be copied into training prompts, targets, or hidden teacher context.

## vLLM adapter loading

The current worker's `export_vllm_adapter()` in [train_worker.py](train_worker.py) implements the required Qwen3.5 hybrid wrapper name repair. It maps PEFT keys beginning `base_model.model.model.layers.*` to `base_model.model.model.language_model.layers.*`, copies all 400 tensors without changing values, verifies the reloaded copy, and writes a mapping receipt. Use the resulting `adapter-vllm/` directory for the vLLM LoRA request. This matches the empirically checked route in [ADAPTER_MAPPING_AUDIT.md](../qwen35-sft-pilot-2026-09-24/ADAPTER_MAPPING_AUDIT.md): the original key layout produced no vLLM log-probability change, while the mapped adapter changed log probabilities on both a training and proof prompt. The unmodified adapter path must not be treated as a valid evaluation result.
