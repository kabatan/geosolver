# Independent read-only audit of `generate_a.py`

Audited `generate(seed=2026092803, per_type=12)` against the original PN01–PN05 questions and their construction verifiers. All 60 targets equal the lengths measured from the returned points; all serialized givens match those points and appear in the English prompts at 17 significant digits. The stated acute, scalene, order, interior, and distinct-intersection branches used by each question hold. Maximum recorded construction/identity residual is `2.25e-12`. No hidden answer relation is used to generate a target: each label comes from circle/line construction, a 120-degree point found by iteration, or a measured distance; solution identities appear only in postconstruction checks.

One diversity issue remains. Distinctness uses a rounded signature set, so near-identical shapes pass. With the seed above, PN02 variants 1 and 5 have normalized side-ratio distance `0.0002010`, and variants 2 and 10 have distance `0.0025162`. PN03 variants 11 and 12 have distance `0.0083368`. This does not invalidate their numeric labels, but reduces shape diversity within the promised 12 variants. A minimum Euclidean distance threshold on full signatures, such as `0.018`, would reject these near duplicates. The PN02 ninth variant selected for separate-shape evaluation is `0.05445` from its closest training variant, so this specific near-duplicate issue does not affect that evaluation split.
