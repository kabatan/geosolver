# Independent audit of shape-residual analysis

The saved formulas reproduce. I checked all 430 positive rows against `fit-scores.json`: `log(y/L)`, `log(pred/L)`, model and `cL` log errors agree exactly; recomputed correlations, pair counts, and squared-log scores differ from the published summaries by at most floating-point rounding (`5.6e-17`). The 30 reserved rows use exactly the same `L` and `c` as the earlier first-length baseline. All three declared input hashes still match. Base's ten nonpositive outputs are correctly excluded from logarithmic metrics; its reduced sample should not be compared directly with the complete SFT arms.

The training correlation of correct512 (`0.946`, 259/280 pair orders) is weighted toward DA07 and DA08: together they provide **70.6% of the centered gold variation** and **71.4% of the prediction variation**. It is not solely their artifact: leaving out either source gives correlation `0.907–0.993`, and leaving out both still gives `0.833` and 204/224 correctly ordered pairs. On reserved shapes, correct512 is much less stable: correlation `0.195`, 19/30 pair orders; leaving out one source gives `0.094–0.351`. Removing both DA07 and DA08 gives correlation `0.578`, but only 16/24 correct pair orders, versus shuffled512's 18/24. The aggregate 19/30 tie between correct512 and shuffled512 hides different pairs: 12 both correct, seven correct512 only, seven shuffled512 only, four both wrong. Each source supplies only three reserved variants, and pair outcomes within a source are dependent.

The drop in correlation cannot be read as a pure loss of geometric skill: the centered gold log-ratio variance per row is `0.284` in training and only `0.0211` in reserve, about **13.5 times narrower**, making Pearson correlation more sensitive to error. The common divisor `L` also enters both normalized quantities; some predictors without a geometric relation can acquire positive normalized correlation, so correlation and pair ordering are behavioral checks, not mechanism identification. The `cL` comparator uses training-only coefficients but the first-length diagnostic was chosen after the reserved results were seen. Correct512's reserved squared-log improvement over that comparator is positive (`0.337`, leave-one-source-out `0.220–0.398`), while shuffled512's is negative (`-0.518`). That evidence argues against claiming that **all** outputs merely memorize size, while zero precise reserved answers and weak normalized ordering also do not establish a reusable exact geometry rule.

Detailed independent recomputation, source-exclusion sensitivities, variance shares, and input hashes are in [shape-residual-review.json](shape-residual-review.json). No model run or change to `shape_residual.py` was made.
