# Projection-bridge evaluation, frozen before training

This is an exploratory positive-control comparison for the three projection-family leave-one-ID-out rotations. It was assembled after the first LOO grades, but before any bridge training or fresh4 answer. The fresh4 target prompts, keys, and construction audits themselves were frozen before the first LOO grades. No fresh4 or fresh2 model answer was opened to author this file.

`eval_jobs.json` contains all six previously audited fresh4 target jobs (two per H03/H04/H14), followed by the four previously audited fresh2 source-retention jobs H01/H02/H07/H13. Its SHA-256 and source input hashes are in `hashes.json`. Target keys remain in `followup-fresh4/answer_key.json`; control keys remain in `followup-fresh2/answer_key.json`. Neither key file enters model context.

For each rotation, train the decomposed treatment and integrated-answer control independently from the same Qwen3.5-9B base with the frozen low-dose recipe: one epoch, LR 1e-5, rank 8/alpha 16, same seed/order/slots. The omitted ID is absent from both training sets. Evaluate the untouched base and **all six** trained arms on the same ten English jobs, unchanged prompts, seeds and sampling (temperature 1, top-p .95, top-k 20, presence penalty 1.5, cap 32768, context 45056). Blind-grade exact value, true useful relation, self-contained decisive proof with branches, and correct use before joining condition labels. In each rotation the primary comparison is the two jobs of its omitted ID; the other four projection jobs are trained-source retention and must not enter the held-out count. Report four source-retention controls separately.

The two fresh4 examples of one omitted ID share its construction and lemma, and H03 includes similarity-scale variants. They are repeated numeric probes, not independent configuration transfer cases. **H03 and H14 are the primary hidden-relation targets:** each requires proving that a circle construction produces the orthocenter projection. H04 explicitly defines its point as the foot, so it is a lower-bar formula-use and branch-completion probe. A promising bridge signal requires an advantage over the matched control on H03 or H14 with the construction-to-projection correspondence actually proved, plus no material retention regression. H04-only improvement does not establish hidden-relation transfer. Even an H03/H14 result remains exploratory and cannot demonstrate discovery without external teachers.

The independent audit of the corrected datasets found treatment/control added-answer token gaps of 5.32–12.26%, while merged-dataset gaps are 0.364–0.923%. For this exploratory comparison, a predeclared ≤2% **merged-dataset** assistant-target-token tolerance is accepted, but this is a format-plus-added-target-length comparison, not strict added-dose matching. Report both gaps and do not attribute a difference solely to decomposition without a tighter follow-up.
