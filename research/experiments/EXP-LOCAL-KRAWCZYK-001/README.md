# EXP-LOCAL-KRAWCZYK-001

Thin local-box Krawczyk rescue over the existing hash-pinned validated typed
DAG. It never subdivides or searches globally. The caller supplies a near-root
and trust radius; a strict one-box certificate may be sent to the unchanged
HP/canonical source, guard, and raw-target replay. Baseline work is fixed at a
minimum of 75%, and all outputs are additive proposals with no authority.

Entrypoint: `candidate.py:rescue`.
