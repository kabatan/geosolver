## 1. Component-local target exactification

The shortest disposable route is not a new algebraic backend.  Reuse
`MSOLVE_EXACT_WITNESS_BIGINT_TRANSPORT_REOPEN_V1`, which already has lossless
BigInt transport and a static upper surface of 20 reversible QQ covers, 18
endpoint-compatible covers, and 9 capped sources in 9 anonymous signatures.
NewRUR/PACE is the same RUR proof state with higher observed startup cost;
alphaCertified proves convergence/realness of a regular numerical root but not
an exact algebraic target descriptor; the existing bounded regular-chain lane
improved 0/61 sources.

Minimum repair, per endpoint:

1. bind immutable source bytes/SHA, full ordinary proposal SHA, endpoint bits,
   compiler/replay ledger SHA, and the exact QQ cover;
2. require one exact RUR factor and one rational isolating box to select a
   unique real parameter root; proximity only orders boxes and is never proof;
3. independently verify the RUR identities and evaluate every coordinate in
   the selected real embedding;
4. run the unchanged QQBar replay of every construction, source equation,
   definedness condition, guard, bound, branch/selector, and raw target;
5. factor the exact raw target polynomial, choose a unique irreducible factor
   by an exact isolator, and recompute the factor-local real-root ordinal;
6. release only that replayed descriptor.  Preserve the numerical baseline,
   set target-image completeness false, and never early-stop from a local root.

This safely handles a target identity valid on one component even when
`T-q` has nonzero remainder modulo the global source ideal.  It is a connector
repair of the existing msolve exact-witness lineage, not a new RUR candidate.

D0 must contain reducible two-target systems, guard-filtered components,
close disjoint boxes, selector branches, and a component-collapsed target;
positive-dimensional, non-radical/repeated, overlapping-box, wrong-factor,
source-mutation, guard, raw-target, and RUR-identity negatives fail closed.
Variable/equation permutation, rational row scaling, and reversible affine
target transforms must preserve the exact descriptor set.  Advance only with
gains on three unrelated generated structures, no false/extra descriptor, and
at least three real source-only conversions in two anonymous signatures from
the already-qualified static panel.

Primary basis: Rouillier-style RURs and exact isolating boxes; the repository's
independent verifier already implements exact quotient identities and Sturm
isolation.  alphaCertified remains only a regular-root existence comparison.

## 2. Universal-rigidity stress certificate

For a bar framework in affine dimension `d`, Connelly's super-stability
sufficient condition requires:

- the candidate coordinates affinely span `R^d`;
- an exact equilibrium stress matrix `Omega` with `Omega*1=0`, `Omega*P=0`,
  zero nonedge entries, `Omega >= 0`, and rank `n-d-1`;
- no nonzero symmetric `d x d` matrix `S` satisfying
  `(p_i-p_j)^T S (p_i-p_j)=0` on every edge.

For bar-only graphs stress-sign conditions are vacuous.  Genericity is not an
assumption of this sufficient theorem and must not be inferred numerically.
The Gortler--Healy--Thurston generic global-rigidity theorem is not a substitute
for universal rigidity of a special algebraic realization.

Every edge length must be an exact consequence with the same value for every
source-valid configuration.  Safe source-only closure admits exact constant
`Distance` equalities; constant-radius `OnCircle`/`PointOnCircle`/circle
intersection edges; equality propagation to an already fixed distance; and
midpoint/reflection consequences only after their parent distance is fixed.
Line incidence, intersection, parallelism, perpendicularity, midpoint, or
circle membership without a fixed radius is not a bar edge.  Fixed anchors may
form their complete exact-distance clique.

The anonymous upper census signature is `(n,m,degree multiset,fixed-anchor
count,edge-provenance histogram,target operator)`.  It first requires a
connected target-bearing graph, a congruence-invariant target, and `m>=2n-3`;
then exact stress/conic checks are later filters.  The explicit-edge quick
ceiling was only 4/56 missing sources, in four singleton shapes, with none
meeting `m>=2n-3`.  Construction-derived fixed-edge closure has not yet proved
the required four sources/two signatures, so the route is `HOLD_CENSUS`.

## 3. Target-cell Positivstellensatz cover

The proposed cell/gap proof is implementable as an exact certificate checker,
but not as a current certificate producer.  It overlaps the archived exact
Positivstellensatz extraneous-root filter directly, and composes that filter
with the existing target-constrained NRA/Hermite existence lanes.  The current
real-radical SOS candidate verifies supplied bounded rational Gram identities
only; its four-source/two-signature producer gate is still unmet.

For finite zero-dimensional sets, existing RUR plus exact Hermite/Tarski sign
queries decides the same cell emptiness more directly.  For genuinely
positive-dimensional inequality sets, no Archimedean hypothesis, practical
degree bound, or exact rational Gram recovery is available.  SDP failure would
not be evidence of feasibility.  Therefore this route is
`STOP_BEFORE_CODE_NO_EXACT_PRODUCER`; reopen only after bounded rational
Positivstellensatz proposals occur on four sources in two signatures and all
descriptor-existence witnesses join independently.

## 6. Round 16 source-height target-zero separation

Arithmetic Nullstellensatz bounds by Krick--Pardo--Sombra are global
certificate/height tools.  Reconstructing a resultant or Groebner basis from
them would duplicate an existing exact backend.  A narrower theorem-level
route remains distinct: the DMM isolated-root separation bound of
Emiris--Mourrain--Tsigaridas applies to an augmented integer system
`f_1=...=f_n=0, y-q(x)=0` and supplies a nonzero lower bound for `|y|` from
source degree, coefficient bits, and mixed-volume or dense bounds, without
constructing a resultant at runtime.

For a Krawczyk-isolated regular root, shift every coordinate exactly away from
zero, prove the raw-target denominator nonzero, and interval-refine `q(X)`.
If `|q(X)|` is strictly below the DMM lower bound, then `q(alpha)=0`.  All
source/guard/branch replay and descriptor irreducibility/ordinal checks remain
mandatory.  The bound is exponentially pessimistic, so status is
`HOLD_SOURCE_PRECISION_CENSUS`: reopen only if at least four sources in two
signatures close within a fixed precision cap.

## 7. Round 17 factorized Hermite fibre

The anonymous degree-one panel gave ten exact-QQ complete typed covers; exactly
one met the old global syntactic Hermite caps.  A relaxed exact-algebraic
coefficient census gave a safe 13-source upper set containing the known eleven
ordinary exact-semialgebraic opportunities.  Every one of the 13
equation-variable incidence graphs was connected.  Hence disconnected product
blocks, strict factorable sources, and per-block cap rescues were all zero.

All 13 systems did have multiple matching-derived block-triangular SCCs, but
these are dependent blocks: downstream equations contain upstream variables.
Independent Hermite nonemptiness counts cannot be multiplied or AND-composed.
Doing so soundly requires ordered algebraic specialization along a triangular
tower, which is LUR/RUR/triangular-decomposition work.  The proposed simple
composition is `STOP_NO_INDEPENDENT_PRODUCT`.  Reopen only with at least four
sources/two signatures having disconnected equation-variable components, a
target local to one component, and every semantic obligation local to exactly
one component.

## 8. Round 18 partial radical border-basis Hermite witness

Akoglu--Szanto reconstructs exact rational Hermite matrices from approximate
roots and verifies multiplication matrices.  Its published complete-ideal
theorem assumes the reconstructed size is at least the quotient dimension;
the paper's root-near-a-point corollary also uses the Hermite matrices of the
whole zero-dimensional ideal.  A proposal cluster must therefore never be
called complete merely because its moments reconstruct.

There is, however, a narrower theorem obligation visible before the equality
step in the proof of their Theorem 4.2.  Let `I=<f>` be the exact source/fibre
ideal.  From a proposal-bound cluster reconstruct a rational extended moment
matrix on a monomial set `B` connected to one.  Exact verification must prove:

1. `H_B` and the extended matrix have the same full rank `k`;
2. the induced rational multiplication matrices `M_i` satisfy the border
   identities and commute;
3. a rational separating combination of the `M_i` has square-free
   characteristic polynomial;
4. every exact source/fibre generator satisfies `f_j(M)=0`;
5. recomputed trace moments agree with the proposed moment matrix.

These checks construct a radical zero-dimensional border-basis ideal `J` with
`I subset J`, hence `V(J) subset V(I)`, without needing `J=I` and without an
all-complex-roots assumption.  Hermite/Tarski sign determination over `J` can
then prove that at least one of those exact source roots lies in a rational
box, satisfies every guard/definedness/branch predicate, and lies in the
proposed target fibre `p(raw_target)=0` and its rational isolator.  Coordinate
minimal polynomials are never constructed; the exact object is the local
quotient representation.  The output is an existence witness only and makes
no target-set completeness claim.

The source-only structural upper gate found 16 complete QQ covers in the
development control proposals; 15 had at least four distinct endpoint hashes
for one exact ordinary descriptor, across 15 anonymous structural signatures.
This is cluster availability, not proof: a real-only subset need not be
Galois-stable, so its moments may not reconstruct over QQ.  Advance only if an
exact reconstruction D0 proves the five obligations and a positive Tarski
count on at least four sources/two signatures under frozen dimension,
denominator, and precision caps.  Any missing conjugates cause fail-closed
rational reconstruction, never cluster padding or an inferred completeness
claim.

This `PARTIAL_RADICAL_BORDER_BASIS_HERMITE` route is mathematically distinct
only in its strict local-subideal authority.  If it computes the complete
source quotient, an eliminant, coordinate RURs, or LUR projection polynomials,
it becomes a stopped global quotient-backend duplicate.  Cheng--Gao--Guo LUR
itself is not a local escape: its algorithm starts with a Groebner basis and a
monomial basis of the full quotient and computes elimination polynomials whose
isolating boxes cover every root.  LUR is therefore `STOP_GLOBAL_GROEBNER_AND_ALL_ROOTS`.
