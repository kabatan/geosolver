# Decision

Status: **`D0_PASS / REAL_AND_COMPILER_HOLD`**

The disposable prototype establishes the proposed causal seam on ten
structurally distinct toy families: four positive-family gains, zero loss,
zero false source replay, and zero truth extra.  Positive, negative, and
near-miss roles cover 4/3/3 independent families, and the required 75% baseline
insurance is invariant.

Do not build a common parameter compiler, fibre solver, continuation backend,
worker, scorer, or telemetry platform from this result.  Real work is held
until an existing source path exposes a low-dimensional parameterized family
and can obtain replicated fibres within the 25% side budget.  The toy use of a
cheap all-endpoint fibre callback is not evidence of real cost or prevalence.

If such a seam exists, the next experiment is one thin candidate-local adapter
and a target-blind activation/source-replay screen.  Otherwise retain this as a
research mechanism and spend no integration work.
