# EFFECTIVE_HEIGHT_SEPARATION_TARGET_CERTIFICATE

## Hypothesis

For a digest-bound exact local source root, suppose every coordinate already
has a primitive irreducible integer minimal polynomial and an exact real-root
isolator.  For an untrusted target descriptor `p`, interval evaluation gives an
upper bound `U` on `|p(raw_target)|`.  Coordinate degree and height bounds give
a computable lower bound `L>0` for the same value if it is nonzero.  Therefore
`U < L` proves exact zero without Gröbner ideal membership.

Repo audit found no implementation of this mechanism.  The nearest earlier
card named a global degree/height contract only as an unimplemented reopen
condition.

## Sound bound used by the prototype

The convention below is the absolute logarithmic Weil height.  Its product,
sum, polynomial-height, Mahler-measure, and Liouville inequalities are covered
in Bombieri and Gubler, *Heights in Diophantine Geometry*, Chapter 1,
especially Sections 1.5--1.7
([Cambridge chapter DOI 10.1017/CBO9780511542879.003](https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511542879.003)).
The polynomial norm/Mahler-measure comparison also goes back to Mahler's
multivariate norm inequalities
([JLMS 37 (1962), DOI 10.1112/jlms/s1-37.1.341](https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms/s1-37.1.341)).

Let coordinate `alpha_i` have primitive irreducible minimal polynomial `q_i`
and let `M_i` be the coefficient L1 norm of `q_i`.  Mahler measure gives

```text
h(alpha_i) <= log(M_i).
```

The joint field degree is at most `D = product degree(q_i)`.  Expand
`p(raw_target) = G(alpha_1,...,alpha_n) / Q`, with integer `G` and positive
integer `Q`.  For `N` monomials, repeated use of
`h(a+b) <= h(a)+h(b)+log(2)` and `h(ab) <= h(a)+h(b)` gives

```text
B = Q * 2^(N-1)
      * product_over_terms(|coefficient|_+ * product_i M_i^exponent_i)
h(p(raw_target)) <= log(B).
```

If the value `beta` were nonzero, its minimal polynomial constant term and the
product over conjugates imply `|beta| >= exp(-deg(beta) h(beta))`.  Hence

```text
|p(raw_target)| >= B^(-D).
```

The prototype accepts only when a rational interval enclosure satisfies the
strict opposite inequality.  It recomputes degree, irreducibility, coefficient
L1 norms, isolators, ordinals, `B`, and `D`; caller-supplied underestimated
height metadata is rejected.

The last inequality can also be checked directly from the primitive minimal
polynomial `a product_j (X-beta_j)`: its nonzero integer constant term has
absolute value at least one, while the other conjugates are bounded by the
terms appearing in `deg(beta) h(beta)`.  Thus this D0 does not rely on a
Dobrowolski/Voutier lower bound, a root-separation heuristic, or an assumed
minimum distance between unrelated algebraic numbers.

## Explicit assumptions required by the theorem

- The coefficient field is exactly `Q`, characteristic zero.  No rounded
  coefficient, `pi`, opaque transcendental, or algebraic coefficient field is
  silently coerced to `Q`.
- Every coordinate polynomial is the positive primitive **irreducible minimal
  polynomial**, not merely an annihilator.  Its rational isolator contains one
  real root, and its zero-based real-root ordinal is recomputed.
- All coordinate facts refer to one common embedding and one common source
  root.  This is not inferred from independent coordinate intervals: a
  digest-bound upstream exact source-root certificate must prove it.
- That upstream certificate also proves every original equation, guard,
  definedness condition, and branch/selector obligation at the same root.
- `raw_target` is a defined rational polynomial on that root.  After clearing
  only constant rational denominators, `p(raw_target)=G/Q` lies in the joint
  number field generated by the certified coordinates.
- The rational coordinate boxes are guaranteed outward enclosures.  Interval
  arithmetic encloses the selected archimedean embedding of `G/Q`; it is never
  allowed to choose a root by proximity.
- `D` is the product of coordinate-minimal-polynomial degrees, hence an upper
  bound on the joint field degree even when coordinates are algebraically
  dependent.  `B>=1` is computed from full expanded integer coefficients; no
  caller-supplied degree or height estimate is trusted.
- The candidate descriptor is itself positive primitive, square-free, and
  irreducible, and its target isolator/ordinal contains the complete raw-target
  interval.  Exact zero membership alone does not establish this selection.
- The proof is local target membership only.  It makes no global target-set
  completeness claim and cannot replace final unchanged canonical replay.

## Authority boundary

- Source, endpoint bits, obligation ledger, lineage, coordinate facts, and
  serialized QQBar records are digest-bound.
- The upstream certificate must prove one same-endpoint source root, all exact
  equations, guards, branches, and same-root coordinate facts.
- The raw target is the source-bound rational polynomial; its full interval
  must lie in the descriptor isolator.
- Numeric proximity is never equality.
- Output is always `authority=false`, `target_release=false`; final unchanged
  canonical source replay is mandatory.

## D0 result

Generated D0 passed **11/11**, with five positives across affine, triangular,
coupled, permutation, and scale structures.  Six negatives cover a nonzero
rational perturbation, wrong factor, underestimated height, singular source,
failed branch replay, and wrong ordinal.  False accepts: **0**.

Next gate is a truth-blind static census of already stored exact coordinate
facts.  The mechanism is useful only when their isolators are narrow enough to
beat the conservative `B^-D` threshold and the old exact target link is absent
or fails.
