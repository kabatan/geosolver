  target factor on a multi-target source may verify the square-free product of
  every independently exact-replayed reservoir factor.  This proves a target
  upper bound without computing a full eliminant.
- Reopen gate: the old exact-link code documents that one `p` returns
  `NOT_PROVED` on multi-target systems.  A reservoir now supplies the missing
  factors, so the proposition itself changes from `p(A)=0` to
  `squarefree(product p_j)(A)=0`.
- Authority: none.  Exact membership is only an upper-bound proposal.
  Per-root exact existence, guards/branches, untouched raw-target replay, and
  canonical descriptor release remain mandatory.
- Custody: factors are canonical primitive positive-leading polynomials and
  must be distinct after unit/scalar normalization.  Every factor has a bound
  descriptor-artifact SHA; source, exact system, descriptor set, and job SHAs
  are passed into the frozen exact-link artifact and returned by the candidate.
- Routing: source equations and already exact-replayed reservoir descriptors
  only.  No ID, family, truth, expected count/value, or prior outcome.
- D0 gate: mixed rational/quadratic components, target-collapsed components,
  positive-dimensional finite image, rational denominator, missing-factor and
  wrong-polynomial negatives.  False accepts must be zero.
- Real gate: at least two exact-replayed distinct reservoir factors; exact
  coefficient/term caps; >=4 sources across >=2 mechanical signatures with a
  verified product identity.  Otherwise STOP.  Saturation/cofactor extraction
  is a later repair only after this direct existing-worker gate activates.
