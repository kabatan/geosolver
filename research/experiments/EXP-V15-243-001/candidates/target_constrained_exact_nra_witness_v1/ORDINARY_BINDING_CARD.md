# Complete ordinary proposal binding connector

## Hypothesis and mechanism

Repair A established a 4/4 thin join from degree-one projections through the
existing primitive cover, exact NRA witness, reconstruction ledger, and full
untouched source DSL replay.  That result could not release a descriptor
because the projection omitted the complete ordinary HP proposal and all
source/endpoint/proposal custody bindings.

This connector adds only that missing seam.  Each selected record binds:

- untouched source bytes and SHA-256;
- the exact big-endian bits and frozen digest of every Float64 endpoint value;
- a primitive leading-positive degree-one polynomial and zero-based root
  ordinal;
- a complete frozen ordinary HP attempt and its artifact SHA-256; and
- the exact ordinary descriptor accepted by the existing digest-pinned
  `exact_outer_decorator/authority_boundary.py`.

The descriptor identity must equal the degree-one identity exactly.  Its
ordinary rational isolator is used by the NRA formula.  After an exact NRA
witness is found, the existing cover reconstruction ledger is recompiled and
the existing full exact source runtime replays the untouched construction,
constraints, bounds, guards, selector/branch ledger, and raw target link.  The
connector returns the original ordinary descriptor without rewriting it.

The frozen current `exact_outer` result stores this proposal under each outer
attempt's `ordinary_hp_proposal`; the outer descriptor is deliberately not
treated as the ordinary proposal.  The source used by the anonymous panel is
the original solve JSON with only its top-level `id` removed and serialized by
the frozen panel builder.  The connector reopens the matching frozen PLAN,
checks the exact endpoint bits, checks the original raw source SHA against the
outer RESULT, recomputes that id-stripped projection byte-for-byte, and binds
it to the anonymous source SHA before exact replay.

## Custody and privacy boundary

The root benchmark owner builds and runs the four-record panel.  Sources are
restricted by resolved path to the anonymous repository-external
`degree1-inputs` root.  Proposal artifacts are restricted to a dedicated
repository-external `ordinary-proposals` root.  Symlink escape, a non-JSON
file, a missing file, or any digest mismatch fails closed.  Source documents
containing ID, family, truth, outcome, score, or known-count keys are rejected
before compilation.

The selected manifest has exactly these fields: relative source path/source
SHA, endpoint Float64 bits/digest, degree-one descriptor, and relative
proposal artifact path/SHA.  It contains no ID, slug, family, truth, answer,
outcome, score, or known target count.  The runner emits individual artifacts
only under the approved repository-external panel root.  Its public result is
fixed aggregate counts plus one ordered binding signature; it exposes no
individual paths, descriptors, targets, witnesses, or failure strings.

## Safety state and generated evidence

The connector proves one emission only and never claims runtime target-set
completeness.  At this experiment stage every connector result has
`authority_pass=false` and `target_release=false`, even when the existing
authority boundary accepts the exact descriptor and all exact replay steps
pass.

The four-request census remains non-authoritative even after the connector
review: successful rows are counted only as `ordinary_binding_pass`,
`exact_replay_pass`, and `readiness`.  Formal authority/release is reserved for
the later current `exact_outer` integration plus canonical scoring gate.

CPU14 generated S0 is `GO_AUDIT`: one complete positive binding passed while
external authority/release remained zero; source, endpoint-bit, descriptor,
and proposal-artifact tampering were rejected 4/4; duplicate matching
ordinary attempts were detected by the builder's exactly-one rule; and a
two-target source admitted one exact emission proof while retaining
`target_set_completeness_claim=false` and `target_release=false`.  The complete
candidate suite passes 34/34.

The earlier Repair A privacy review also closed its source-path oracle: the
degree-one census now restricts sources to the anonymous panel root and
rejects source identity/truth fields before compiler/replay.

## Original-result provenance and TCB

The copied proposal artifact binds the SHA-256 of its frozen formal-control
`RESULT.json`.  To avoid materializing case-bearing paths, the connector scans
only regular `RESULT.json` files under the approved control root and requires
that the digest identify exactly one original.  It then requires exactly one
matching source/endpoint/descriptor authority-ready attempt and canonical
equality with the copied attempt.  The exact-coordinate
`nested_exact_authority_matches_source` helper is intentionally not invoked:
it accepts a different exact-coordinate fallback schema, while source
authority here is supplied by the fresh NRA witness plus full exact DSL replay.

The one-shot builder and write-protected custody directories are part of the
trusted benchmark-owner TCB.  Hashes cannot authenticate an adversary able to
rewrite both an artifact and every attestation supplied to the same process.
The connector instead prevents caller-selected file escape and revalidates all
copied ordinary evidence against the frozen original root.
