# Repair A — real emitter readiness

## Decision: STOP real Gram connector

The aggregate-only emitter gate failed: `3 attempts / 3 anonymous signatures`
against the fixed minimum `4 / 2`. The three ready attempts are source-labelled
circle-circle Henneberg steps; the remaining twelve eligible sources leave one
or two free points unresolved by two exact labelled loci.

No endpoint value was used to fill the missing EDM. In particular, Float64
endpoint distances were not recognized by PSLQ and never became authority.
Endpoint use remains limited to deterministic branch trial order.

The readiness scanner admits a free point only when it has two already-exact
source-labelled line/circle loci. Exact anchors and deterministic construction
dependencies are closed first; branch growth is capped at 32. A future connector
would still need to enumerate and canonically replay every exact branch.

Because the gate failed, no real OMC source emitter or Gram connector was
implemented. The shared exact construction nucleus moved to the separate
`SOURCE_LABELLED_RULER_COMPASS_WITNESS` generated D0 instead.

The census is truth-free and aggregate-only. It emits no individual ID, value,
target, truth, readiness result, or outcome. Nondevelopment rows are discarded
before JSON deserialization. No formal execute, scorer, full98, or CPU other
than CPU15 was used.
