# Mechanism and overlap boundary

The existing direct and radical-square candidates lower only a conservative
closed-affine subset of the source and target.  Repair C instead consumes the
frozen typed compiler's complete equation ledger, including target-created
auxiliaries, and refuses to omit any equation.  It is therefore a compiler
coverage repair, not a larger Groebner search, coefficient/degree tuning, or
case-specific symbolic template.

It does not replace source-root certification, branch/guard checking,
canonical source replay, target descriptor authority, or baseline union.  It
does not handle selectors or reconstruction.  Development overlap has a
truth-blind ceiling of three sources, so this version remains stopped after
generated evidence.  Reopen only after an overdetermined exact source-root
certificate is independently available and an anonymous aggregate establishes
a ceiling above the frozen promotion gate.
