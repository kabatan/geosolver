# Overlap and theorem boundary

Repair B retains the exact theorem from the direct and radical-square
candidates.  Its only new mechanism is bounded selection from a larger ledger
of independently safe source equality candidates.  It does not broaden the
expression language, selected proof size, radical power, cofactor degree,
polynomial degree/term cap, or timeout.

This is distinct from full-source Gröbner elimination and from the typed root
solver.  The selected ideal `I_S` may omit most source equalities.  That is
sound for the one-way implication because every selected generator is still
a source consequence.  Omitted constraints cannot make an unproved identity
pass; the synthetic omitted-only negative checks that boundary.

Reopen a three-way experimental join only when the aggregate census shows a
nonzero intersection between a Repair B identity and the independently
audited full-source typed root compiler.  A complete ordinary proposal and an
independently audited replay seam remain mandatory before any authority
discussion.
