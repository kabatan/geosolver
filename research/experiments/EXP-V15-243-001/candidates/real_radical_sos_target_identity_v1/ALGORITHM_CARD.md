# REAL_RADICAL_SOS_TARGET_IDENTITY_V1 — synthetic D0

- Hypothesis: an exact real-Nullstellensatz identity `h^(2m) + z^T Q z = Σ a_i g_i` can prove that raw target offset `h=raw_target-q` vanishes on every real equality root even when both `h` and `h²` fail ordinary ideal-membership tests.
- Mechanism: accept only `m∈{1,2}`, rational polynomial generators/cofactors, monomial SOS basis of degree ≤3, and a symmetric rational Gram matrix whose every principal minor is nonnegative. Verify the identity over `QQ`, reject the unit ideal, and retain direct/`h²` membership only as an ablation. The identity is a side-link: it never emits authority by itself and requires separate real-root existence plus full guard/branch/source replay certificates.
- Research basis: Lasserre–Laurent–Rostalski, *Semidefinite Characterization and Computation of Real Radical Ideals* ([arXiv:math/0609528](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0609528)); Lasserre–Laurent–Mourrain–Rostalski–Trébuchet, *Moment Matrices, Border Bases and Real Radical Computation* ([arXiv:1112.3197](https://arxiv.org/abs/1112.3197)). V1 verifies supplied bounded exact witnesses; it does not implement the papers' full moment hierarchy.
- D0: 1,280 synthetic instances, seed 731904, CPU14. Three positives are `I=<x²+y²>, h=x`, a rational rotated/scaled form, and a two-generator chain. Seven negatives cover wrong `q`, complex-only/no-existence join, unit ideal, omitted generator, tampered Gram, inequality-lane input, and opaque transcendental input. Four variants cover variable permutation/renaming, equation permutation plus rational row scaling, and reversible affine target transformation.
- Result: all three positive families join at rate 1.0 while direct and square membership remain 0.0; complex-only identities verify but never join; all other negatives reject. False authority, negative join, and metamorphic failures are all zero.
- Decision: **ADVANCE** only to a truth-blind eligibility/proposal census. Synthetic-only; no formal corpus, truth, 98-case holdout, 145/243 score, or production claim.

Reopen/continue only if exact bounded witness proposal can be produced on at least four eligible source instances across two source hashes, and each identity can be joined to an independently verified real-root existence proof and complete guard/branch/source replay. Do not expand degree/moment bounds by parameter sweep without such activation evidence.
