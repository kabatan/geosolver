seven gain structures), but failed its immediate gates through nine root-class
losses and 54 metamorphic mismatches: translation 34, coordinate scale 17,
and periodic chart 3.  Its novelty score used absolute-coordinate distance and

the source residual, local solver, prefix generation, and independent source

## Protected portfolio and invariant score

Ordinals not congruent to 3 modulo 4 are the protected control portfolio:
75% of starts remain byte-identical at the same launch.  Only the 25% side
class (`ordinal mod 4 == 3`) may permute among itself, so adaptive selection
can never consume or displace a protected ordinal.

The score never reads coordinates.  It combines:

- a multiset of failed typed GuardIR leaf kinds, excluding source paths/keys;
- signed-margin columns standardized from the untouched prefix;
- absolute source-residual columns standardized from the same prefix; and
- a prefix covariance whitening transform.

Repeated same-kind margins and residual rows are sorted after per-column
standardization.  Therefore translation/periodic coordinate shifts disappear,
positive coordinate/guard units and equation units cancel against prefix
scales, and row/key permutations do not define novelty.  Rank-deficient
prefix directions receive zero weight.  Any nonfinite or incomplete feature
path, or an archive fitted to different prefix feature observations, fails
closed to exact control order.

## Fixed generated gate

Before any real screen, the old public panel must run all 1,440 paired trials
with zero losses, false roots, protected-ordinal failures, permutation
failures, or inactive runs; at least two structures must gain; CPU ratio must
not exceed 1.15.  Translation and coordinate-scale transformations cover all
eight structures, three strata, two constants, and three seeds.  Periodic
chart covers the periodic structure on the same strata/constants/seeds.  All
three mandatory metamorphic kinds require 100% root-set parity.

This commit is a static, unexecuted prototype.  It has no authority or target
release and is not authorized for a real screen until the fixed generated
gate passes.
