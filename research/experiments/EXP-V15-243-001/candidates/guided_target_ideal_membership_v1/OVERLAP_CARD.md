# Overlap and theorem boundary

## Existing coverage

`wave12_modular_source_target_link_v2` already supplies a proposal-only
finite-field/CRT acceleration for a complete polynomial source-to-target link.
Its source-only upper bound was 9 eligible structures / 7 clusters, and every
authority remains delegated to the existing exact verifier.

`certified_regular_root_wave2` already combines a bounded regular-root theorem,
strict guards, full source ideal membership, and localized target-root
selection.  It is complete within its small QQ polynomial scope.  Replacing
its Groebner backend, interval contractor, or target factor schedule would be
duplicate work.

`typed_guard_algebraic_extension` plus its fresh v2 validator independently
approved a broader nonrecursive source-root and typed-guard compiler, but
explicitly stopped standalone target authority.  Recursive/disjunctive guard
extension remains HOLD.

The Wave 13 proof-conversion audit concluded that incomplete proof halves must
not be joined by self-attested flags.  A root enclosure and exact target link
are distinct mandatory conjuncts.

## Narrow experimental difference

This v1 does not construct a complete source ideal or a new root certificate.
Its sole mathematical experiment is a universal target identity derived from
a lowerable equality subset, with a direct source-equation cofactor witness.
Unlike component-local factor selection, the proved identity applies to every
solution of the full source because all adopted equations are source
consequences.  Root existence and all nonrecursive guards come exclusively
from the audited typed certificate; the unique target ordinal comes from a
primitive degree-one relation.

This difference can expand target-link syntax coverage only where an audited
typed source-root certificate already exists.  Without that certificate the
terminal is HOLD.  If the existing regular-root route already has its own exact
target link, this experiment is a duplicate and provides no incremental value.

## Non-overclaim

- No singular-root theorem.
- No target-set completeness theorem.
- No recursive guard support.
- No selector, sqrt/sign, division, or squared-distance relaxation.
- No authority emission or common worker integration.
- No claim that the 29/135 readiness upper bound joins with the separately
  observed degree-one ordinary proposals.

Reopen common integration only after an outcome-blind join census demonstrates
enough attempts/signatures and an independent audit approves the exact
three-way binding seam.
